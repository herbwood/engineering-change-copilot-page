06 / ENGINEERING CHALLENGES

The hard part is the evidence.

01

Identity across temporary reference drift

Combine assembly lineage, native references and qualified hierarchy/configuration evidence. Preserve original observations; withhold ambiguous pairings.

02

Conservative conclusions from incomplete data

Separate known suppression from unavailable properties. Distinguish FAIL from INCONCLUSIVE. Keep independently authored requirements separate from observed CAD.

03

From AI citations to an actual occurrence

Issue context-bound evidence handles, reject forged/stale links, verify current native identity and check selection/camera postconditions.

OPEN LIMITATION

Historical suppression capture discrepancy

The user observed an unsuppressed target, while its original saved baseline says suppressed. The diagnostic lookup gate has been corrected; post-fix native acceptance and historical capture accuracy remain unverified. The demo does not resolve this issue.
